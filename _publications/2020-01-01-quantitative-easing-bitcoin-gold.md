Original title: *Pengaruh Kebijakan Quantitative Easing Amerika Serikat terhadap Harga Bitcoin dan Emas pada Periode 2012-2019*. Supervisor: Rimawan Pradiptyo, S.E., M.Sc., Ph.D.

The thesis uses a Vector Error Correction Model (VECM) on 368 weekly observations from December 2012 to December 2019 to study how US Quantitative Easing affects the prices of bitcoin and gold. Gold and bitcoin respond differently to QE shocks: the results suggest bitcoin is not a safe-haven asset, and that its price rises are driven mostly by demand shocks for bitcoin itself.
