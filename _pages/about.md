I tinker with computers. I bring old software to new platforms and squeeze more out of the hardware I own.

I hold a Bachelor of Economics and have co-authored economic research articles on banking, digital currency and inflation in Indonesia (see [Research](/research/)).

Most of my open-source work falls into two areas:

**Game preservation & porting.** I port classic games to modern platforms:

- **MultiHero** (the Blitz3D 2D fighting game): a transpiler from BlitzBasic to C++, an [Android port](https://github.com/richardyusan/multihero-android), a [WebAssembly build that runs in the browser](https://richardyusan.github.io/multihero-web/), and [online multiplayer](https://github.com/richardyusan/multihero-online) via Epic Online Services with its own [server](https://github.com/richardyusan/multihero-server).
- **ManiaDrive**: an [Android port](https://github.com/richardyusan/maniadrive-android) of the 2006 acrobatic racer, built on the Raydium 3D engine with OpenGL ES 2 via gl4es.

**Laptop & firmware tooling.** I write tools for the Lenovo IdeaPad 14ALC6 (Ryzen 5500U) and AMD desktops: a [Windows toolkit](https://github.com/richardyusan/ideapad-14alc6-toolkit), [fan control](https://github.com/richardyusan/ideapad-fan-control), [battery conservation mode](https://github.com/richardyusan/ideapad-conservation-mode), Linux Wi-Fi power tuning, OpenCore Hackintosh EFIs, and a utility that finds what is [keeping a monitor awake](https://github.com/richardyusan/monitor-sleep-blockers).

**Tech I work with:** C, C++, C#, Python, JavaScript/Node.js, BlitzBasic, ACPI/ASL, Android NDK, OpenGL ES, Emscripten/WebAssembly.
